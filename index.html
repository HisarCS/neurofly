Mechanical Architecture & Duct Physics

The airframe pairs a 2D laser-cut Baltic birch or acrylic spine (85mm diagonal wheelbase) with 3D-printed TPU flexible propeller ducts.

  • Center of Gravity (CoG): Symmetrically aligned with central LiPo tray beneath the frame.
  • Weight Budget: Bare airframe = 4.8g; Prop ducts = 1.4g each; AUW with 1S LiPo < 42g.
  • Impact Resilience: 95A TPU ducts absorb up to 15 km/h wall impacts without fracturing.

Carrier Board & Power Switching

Designed for fabrication on FabLab CNC PCB mills (Roland SRM-20) or standard PCB fab.

  • Processor: Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6 (32-bit RISC-V at 160MHz).
  • Motor Drivers: 4x AO3400A N-Channel MOSFETs with 100Ω gate resistors and 10kΩ pull-downs.
  • Inductive Protection: 4x SS14 Schottky flyback diodes clamping back-EMF spikes.
  • Sensors: I2C bus connecting MPU6050 6-DOF IMU and optional VL53L0X ToF laser ranger.
