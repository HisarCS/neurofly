The Open-Source Indoor Drone & STEM Platform

NeuroFly brings together digital fabrication, embedded 32-bit RISC-V systems (XIAO ESP32-C6), low-latency wireless telemetry (ESP-NOW), and interactive browser-based 3D simulation for hands-on aerial robotics education.

Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6

High-efficiency 32-bit RISC-V core operating at 160MHz with native Wi-Fi 6, BLE 5.3, and ESP-NOW low-latency RF protocol for deterministic motor PWM control.

FabLab Digital Fabrication

Custom parametric laser-cut acrylic/wood chassis paired with 3D-printed flexible TPU propeller ducts ensuring classroom-safe indoor flight under 45 grams AUW.

Hardware-in-the-Loop Sim

Zero-install Web Serial API allows students to plug their physical remote controller directly into Chrome or Edge to practice gate racing and test control algorithms.

KiCad PCB Design & Architecture

The carrier board integrates four logic-level AO3400 N-channel MOSFETs, SS14 flyback protection diodes, an I2C socket for MPU6050 6-DOF IMUs, and a voltage divider for live telemetry battery tracking.

WEEK 1

Digital Fabrication

Laser cut airframes (kerf adjustment) and 3D print flexible TPU prop ducts. Mass budgeting under 45g.

WEEK 2

Electronics & Soldering

SMD soldering (0805 passives & SOT-23 MOSFETs), Roland SRM-20 PCB milling, and short-circuit verification.

WEEK 3

Firmware & Simulator

ESP32-C6 ESP-NOW protocol, transmitter assembly with OLED, and Hardware-in-the-Loop 3D simulation training.

WEEK 4

Grand Prix Challenge

PID tuning, 3-minute gate racing, autonomous altitude hold, and AI vision target landing.
