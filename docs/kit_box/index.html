Kit box · v0.3.0

Open the box.
Four levels are waiting inside.

Every NeuroFly team gets a laser-cut box that opens like a reveal: slide the lid, lift each layer by its ribbon, and find the parts, the missions and a few hidden messages. Built in a school FabLab from plywood or acrylic, paper and a 3D printer.

Start unboxing Download the laser files Print the cards
  • Press-fit finger joints, no glue for the shell
  • 3 mm plywood or acrylic, 400 × 300 mm sheets
  • 4 levels: Build, Wire, Fly, Code
  • Optional LED lid

Make the box

Laser files, generated by one script

Seven 400 × 300 mm sheets cut one box. Sheet 1 holds the lid, the base and three walls; the fourth wall rides on sheet 2 with the reveal plates. Sheets 3 to 7 hold the four trays. Every cut is a closed vector path.

Red: cut Blue: vector engrave Black: raster engrave Grey notes: hide, do not cut

    Default parameters

    Material3.0 mm plywood or cast acrylic (measure it)
    Kerf0.15 mm, every path offset by half
    Press fit0.05 mm (use 0 for acrylic)
    Outer size210 × 140 × 65.25 mm
    LidSlides forward in slots in the side walls
    TraysLift-out layers on ribbons

    Starting settings (CO2, 50–80 W)

    MaterialCut
    Baltic birch 3 mm18–22 mm/s, 65–75 %
    Cast acrylic 3 mm12–15 mm/s, 70–80 %
    Vector engrave (blue)Same speed or faster, low power
    Raster engrave (black)Fast, low power, tune on scrap

    Test on scrap first. These are starting points from the repository README, not measured values for your machine. Cut a 20 mm test square and a finger-joint test before you cut the real sheets. Do engraving before cutting, and never leave a running laser unattended.

    Assemble in six moves

    1. Press the base into the front, back and two side walls. The finger joints are press-fit; tap with a flat block, no glue needed.
    2. Check the slots in the side walls are open towards the front and the lid slides without binding. Rub a candle stub on the slot if needed.
    3. Glue each tray: stack its pocket plates on the floor plate with the same orientation and press until dry. The top plate carries the engraving.
    4. Tape a ribbon under each floor so both tails fold up into the side gaps.
    5. Drop the trays in: Code at the bottom, then Fly, Wire, Build, and the reveal on top.
    6. Slide the lid in from the front, add the welcome card and the team number.

    Card stock and vinyl

    Print the cards below on 250–300 gsm card, or on sticker paper and stick them on thin board. Use a vinyl cutter for the team number on the lid and a two-colour NeuroFly logo for the front wall.

    Printables
